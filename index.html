What it does

One app, the whole stack

Every surface is built around the same idea: your data stays on your hardware, and the app is just a secure, fast window into it.

🎥

Sub-second live view

Native WebRTC with a per-session relay for low latency, plus automatic HLS fallback so there is always a picture — even on a rough cellular link.

⏪

Scrubbable DVR

A real timeline: drag through a week of continuous recording, see exactly where footage exists, and jump to any instant — with audio.

🔊

Camera audio

Live and recorded audio, transcoded on the fly so it plays everywhere — WebRTC, HLS, and saved clips alike.

🛡️

DNS filtering

AdGuard at your fingertips: a trustworthy query log, allow/block rules, client management, and one-tap protection pauses.

📊

System health

A live dashboard of your services and hosts, so you know the state of the whole box from the home screen.

⚙️

Edge configuration

Manage your reverse proxy and identity provider safely from the app, behind guard rails and an audit trail.

💾

Storage & retention

See what recordings actually cost against a quota — not the whole disk — and tune retention without new privileges.

⭐

Favourites & order

Pin the cameras you care about and arrange them your way, synced across the app.

🔐

Least-privilege access

Role-based permissions enforced server-side, biometric confirmation for sensitive actions, and short-lived, scoped playback tokens.

How it fits together

A thin, secure window over your own media plane

The app never talks to your cameras or services directly. A gateway you control mediates everything — authorizing each request, minting short-lived tokens, and keeping internal services off the public internet.

Recent releases

What's new

The last stretch has been about making the fast path reliable — smoother video, trustworthy data, and releases that can't ship broken.

0.2.5latest

Backend hot-path and upstream hardening, and CI that publishes only the intended release artifacts.

0.2.4streaming

Adaptive 1080p streaming — the live view scales to the connection instead of stalling on a fixed bitrate.

0.2.0 – 0.2.3reliability

WebRTC TURN fallback prioritization, deinit-safe stream observation, clearer "gateway unreachable" states, and serialized, validated iOS releases that block on real device-test lanes.

0.2.1adguard

Made AdGuard query activity trustworthy — precise allowed/blocked/unknown classification instead of guesswork.

0.1.7 – 0.1.9foundation

Smoother camera playback, AdGuard usability, and broad reliability improvements across the app.

0.1.xthe big build-out

Native WebRTC player with automatic HLS fallback, camera audio (AAC transcode), the scrubbable recordings timeline, offline-camera handling, tolerant permission decoding, an infrastructure-management screen, and reliable AltStore delivery.
