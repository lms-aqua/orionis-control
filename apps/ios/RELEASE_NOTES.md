Orionis Control 0.2.3 focuses on live-camera playback resilience and diagnosability. WebRTC now detects sustained severe frame-rate collapse using a per-camera healthy baseline, ignores brief dips and naturally low-rate sources, performs one bounded fast renegotiation, and falls back to HLS if the replacement peer remains degraded instead of leaving a one-frame-per-second stream marked live. Camera changes, backgrounding, and network transitions reset the detector, while the playback HUD exposes the measured baseline and low-FPS recovery count.

When live media degrades, the app now submits a best-effort, throttled incident report to the authenticated gateway. Negotiation failures, dropped peers, missing first frames, frozen video, low FPS, HLS stalls or failures, and exhausted recovery are written to the gateway's structured server log and durable administrator audit log with bounded playback metrics, device context, and camera identity. Reports never include SDP, media URLs, stream tokens, TURN credentials, or arbitrary raw errors. Intake is schema-validated and rate-limited, and only administrators can read the durable reports.
