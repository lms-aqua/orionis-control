Orionis Control 0.2.1 makes the AdGuard query log trustworthy and easier to investigate. Filtering outcomes now use AdGuard Home's exact documented reasons instead of broad substring guesses, unfamiliar future reasons stay neutral, and each query exposes the original AdGuard reason in its detail view. Counts are explicitly labelled as the latest loaded sample, an all-blocked sample explains that it is not an all-time total, and you can load 100 older queries at a time or share a domain directly. This release also keeps the 0.2.0 camera, playback, WebRTC, authentication, stream-security, stale-state, and gateway-error hardening, and ensures the rolling AltStore tag moves to the exact commit that passed iOS CI.
